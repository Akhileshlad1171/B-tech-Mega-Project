# Vertical Axis Wind Turbine (VAWT) for Highway Energy Generation

## Overview
Vertical Axis Wind Turbines (VAWTs) represent a unique form of power-generating technology. While they have historically been used in niche markets due to their "yaw-less" design, recent research suggests that VAWTs may be better suited for wind farm installations than previously thought.

This project aims to utilize wind energy effectively by installing VAWTs along highways, where moving vehicles can generate additional airflow, enhancing energy output. The power generated will be stored in a battery and used for applications such as street lighting, traffic signals, and toll booths.

## System Architecture
The VAWT system consists of:
1. **Turbine Design:**
   - Semi-circular blades connected to a disc
   - Disc mounted on a shaft for rotation
   - Shaft connected to a pulley via bearings
2. **Power Generation:**
   - Pulley drives an alternator to generate electricity
   - Generated power is stored in a battery
3. **Power Utilization:**
   - Stored energy can be used for:
     - Street lights
     - Traffic signals
     - Toll booths

## Hardware Components
- **Vertical Axis Wind Turbine (VAWT)** – Captures wind energy
- **Alternator** – Converts mechanical energy into electrical energy
- **Battery** – Stores generated power
- **Microcontroller (AT89C52)** – Manages system operations
- **LCD Display** – Displays system data
- **Relay Module** – Controls power distribution
- **ESP8266** – Enables wireless communication
- **ADC 0808** – Analog-to-digital conversion for sensor readings
- **Voltage Sensor** – Monitors voltage levels
- **Relay Driver** – Controls relay operation

## Features
- **Renewable Energy Source:** Utilizes wind energy efficiently
- **Highway-Based Installation:** Leverages vehicle-induced wind flow
- **Cost-Effective:** Designed for maximum output at minimal cost
- **Battery Storage:** Enables consistent power availability
- **Scalability:** Can be implemented on multiple highways

## Installation & Setup
1. **Hardware Assembly:**
   - Install the turbine and connect it to the alternator
   - Set up battery storage and power distribution system
2. **Microcontroller Programming:**
   - Program AT89C52 for system monitoring and control
   - Configure ESP8266 for remote monitoring
3. **Testing & Calibration:**
   - Verify power generation efficiency
   - Monitor voltage levels and battery charging
